How it works

Ready in four steps

  1. Install GestureBoard

    Download the installer and set it up on your Windows PC or laptop.

  2. Choose your camera

    Use the built-in webcam, or connect your phone by scanning the QR code.

  3. Open your presentation

    Start your slideshow in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or a PDF viewer.

  4. Teach with gestures

    Change slides and move the cursor with your hand while GestureBoard tracks it in real time. Use your mouse to annotate or open the whiteboard.

Gesture guide

Learn the gestures

Hand gestures control the slides and the virtual mouse. Hold your hand clearly in front of the camera, about an arm's length away.

    Swipe right

    Next slide

  • 👈

    Swipe left

    Previous slide

  • ☝️

    Index finger up

    Move the cursor (virtual mouse)

  • 🤏

    Pinch

    Click

  • ✌️

    Two fingers up

    Switch mode

  • ✋

    Open palm

    Pause gesture control

Phone as camera

No webcam? Use your phone.

You don't need to install an app. It works on Android and iPhone through the phone's browser.

  1. Connect both devices. Put your phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi, use your phone's hotspot, or use USB tethering.
  2. Scan the QR code shown in GestureBoard using your phone's camera.
  3. Allow camera access when the browser asks. That's it, your phone is now the camera.

Install guide

Installing GestureBoard

Windows may show a blue warning screen the first time. This is normal for new apps from small developers.

  1. Download the installer

    Click Download for Windows and open GestureBoard_Setup_1.0.0.exe from your Downloads folder.

  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC"

    Click More info.

    Windows protected your PC

    Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting…

    More info

  3. Click "Run anyway"

    The button appears at the bottom after clicking More info.

    App: GestureBoard_Setup_1.0.0.exe

    Run anywayDon't run

  4. Click Next

    Follow the setup wizard. You can keep the default install location.

  5. Click Install

    Wait for it to finish, then open GestureBoard from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is GestureBoard free?

Yes. GestureBoard is completely free to download and use.

Does it need internet?

No. You only need internet to download the installer. After that, GestureBoard runs fully offline. If you use your phone as the camera, the phone and PC only need to be on the same local network (Wi-Fi, hotspot, or USB tethering). That network doesn't need internet access.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. GestureBoard is made for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only.

Is it safe? Why does Windows show a warning?

Yes. The "Windows protected your PC" message appears because the app is new and isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. It doesn't mean the app is harmful. GestureBoard doesn't collect or upload your camera feed, because all processing happens on your computer. The source code is public on GitHub.

Can I draw or write with hand gestures?

No. Hand gestures are for changing slides and controlling the virtual mouse. Digital annotation and the virtual whiteboard are used with your regular mouse.

Can I use it with PowerPoint and Google Slides?

Yes. Gesture slide control sends standard next/previous keystrokes, so it works with most slideshow apps, including PowerPoint, Google Slides in a browser, and PDF viewers.
